Discover the Magic

Where Three Mountain Streams Converge

The name Munnar originates from the Malayalam words Moonu (three) and Aaru (river), marking the confluence of the Mudhirapuzha, Nallathanni, and Kundala rivers.

From the second-highest peak in the Western Ghats at Meesapulimala to the world's highest tea plantation at Kolukkumalai, Munnar offers unmatched biodiversity, spice-scented mountain air, and hiking trails that leave you spellbound.

  • Endangered Nilgiri Tahr and purple Neelakurinji blooms
  • Rugged 4x4 Jeep safaris over cloud-shrouded ridges
  • Organic orthodox tea factories operating since 1935
  • Serene lake boat rides, arch dams, and hidden waterfalls

Expert Planning

The Ultimate 4-Day Munnar Expedition

Carefully crafted by TrackWorld guides who have lived in these hills for generations. Designed for minimal driving, maximum exploration, and unforgettable sunrises.

Day 01

Arrive, Tea Estates & Golden Sunset

Route: Scenic Drive via Cheeyappara ➔ Tea Museum ➔ Pothamedu Sunset Viewpoint ➔ Campfire Welcome

Ascend the mist-wrapped Western Ghats from Cochin. Freshen up with authentic cardamom tea, explore the 140-year colonial legacy at Tata Tea Museum, and watch the sun dip into the rolling emerald tea ridges of Pothamedu.

Morning: Arrival & Falls Stop at Cheeyappara 7-tier falls for photos and hot fresh chai.
Afternoon: Tea History Live tea plucking and artisanal factory tour at Nallathanni Estate.
Evening: Golden Sunset Hike to Pothamedu Ridge followed by traditional Kerala dinner.
Day 02

Kolukkumalai Sunrise 4x4 & Meesapulimala Trails

Route: Suryanelli ➔ 4x4 Off-Road Safari ➔ World's Highest Tea Plantation ➔ Rhodo Valley Ridge Hike

A 4:30 AM thrilling off-road jeep ascent through rugged mountain tracks to Kolukkumalai. Gaze in awe as the sun breaks above an endless sea of clouds, then trek the high ridges surrounded by blooming high-altitude wildflowers.

4:30 AM: Jeep Safari Thrilling 4x4 climb through misty tea estates in the dark.
6:15 AM: Sea of Clouds Watch the world awaken above 2,400 m with fresh mountain chai.
Afternoon: Ridge Trek Trek the lower ridges of Meesapulimala through pine groves.
Day 03

Lakes, Natural Echoes & Top Station Border Ridge

Route: Mattupetty Dam ➔ Echo Point ➔ Kundala Arch Dam ➔ Top Station Cloud Valley

Follow the high mountain highway bordering evergreen forests and reservoirs. Try speedboating at Mattupetty, call into the acoustic canyon at Echo Point, glide on a Kashmiri Shikara at Kundala, and finish at the cliff edges of Top Station.

Morning: Speedboat Safari Cruising Mattupetty lake with chances of spotting wild elephants.
Noon: Echoes & Shikara Natural resonance at Echo Point and peaceful Shikara boat ride.
Sunset: Top Station Stand on the Kerala-Tamil Nadu edge overlooking 360° cloud seas.
Day 04

Nilgiri Tahr Sanctuary, Lakkam Falls & Marayoor

Route: Eravikulam National Park ➔ Anamudi View ➔ Lakkam Waterfalls ➔ Sandalwood & Departure

Spot the rare Nilgiri Tahr mountain goats on the shola slopes of Rajamalai, take a refreshing foot dip in the crystal plunge pool of Lakkam Falls, and stock up on pure first-flush orthodox tea and Kerala spices before heading home.

Morning: Wildlife Safari Early safari at Eravikulam to observe wild Nilgiri Tahr herds.
Noon: Lakkam Spring Dip Cool mountain plunge and stroll under ancient Vaga tree canopy.
Afternoon: Spice & Tea Market Handpicked fresh cardamom, cinnamon, and artisanal green teas.
When to Visit

Munnar Through the Seasons

Every month brings a fresh personality to Munnar's hills. Find the perfect season for your travel style.

October – February

Winter & Mist

10°C – 20°C

Peak travel season. Crisp starry nights, thick morning fog, and crystal-clear sunrise views above cloud carpets.

  • Best for high-peak sunrise treks
  • Pleasant daytime walking weather
  • Stargazing and bonfire nights
March – May

Summer Retreat

18°C – 26°C

The perfect cool refuge from the hot plains of South India. Tea bushes sprout fresh delicate light-green shoots.

  • Ideal for family holidays & boating
  • Clear trekking trails
  • Fresh tea leaf harvest season
June – August

Monsoon Rhapsody

15°C – 21°C

The hills turn an electric emerald green. Waterfalls thunder down cliffs, and romantic clouds float right through your balcony.

  • Waterfalls at maximum roar
  • Peaceful, uncrowded atmosphere
  • Ayurvedic rejuvenation stays
September – October

Post-Monsoon Bloom

14°C – 22°C

The rains wash the sky pristine blue. Wild mountain orchids and wildflowers burst into bloom along all trails.

  • Vibrant floral landscapes
  • Crystal clear photography light
  • Ideal camping temperatures
Local Expert Advice

Essential Tips for Your Munnar Journey

Make the most of your hill station escape with these insider tips from our local guides.

🚗 How to Reach

Cochin International Airport (COK) is 110 km (3.5 hours) away via NH 85. Aluva is the nearest major railway station (115 km). TrackWorld provides private sanitized transfers.

🎒 What to Pack

Bring thermal layers for early morning sunrises (temperatures dip to 6°C), sturdy hiking shoes with grip, rain jacket, reusable water bottle, and binoculars for birdwatching.

🛍️ What to Buy

First-flush orthodox Black and Green tea, fresh green cardamom, natural eucalyptus oil, homemade dark chocolates, and Marayoor sugarcane jaggery.
