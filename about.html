Our Roots

How TrackWorld Began

TrackWorld started 14 years ago with weekend hikes for close friends who wanted to experience Munnar beyond standard tourist viewpoints.

Today, we have guided over 5,000 trekkers across Meesapulimala, Kolukkumalai, and Top Station. Yet, our founding principle has never changed: preserve the mountain wilderness, support local tribal families, and never sacrifice the magic of a small intimate trail group.

  • Wilderness First-Aid Certified: Every trail guide is trained in mountain emergency response.
  • Strict Leave-No-Trace Ethics: We carry out everything we bring in and organise monthly trail cleanups.
  • Direct Community Impact: 70% of tour revenue goes directly to local homestays, jeep drivers, and forest guides.
